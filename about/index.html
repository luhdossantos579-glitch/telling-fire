About The Telling Fire

The Telling Fire retells Norse, Greek, Egyptian, Celtic and world myths as stories, names the oldest sources, and shows where each myth lives today.

Who makes this

The Telling Fire is a storytelling channel on YouTube, published by Vision Media. We research the old texts and write every retelling ourselves. The storyteller's voice is text-to-speech, and every video description tells you so.

How we work

Each tale follows a source we name (the Prose Edda, Ovid, a translated hymn), and we say where the versions disagree. You'll find those texts in each video's description and at the end of every article. The illustrations and images are public domain, openly licensed or our own; the credits page gives them for each video.
